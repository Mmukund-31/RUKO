# RUKO — Product Spec

**Tagline:** Pause. Verify. Stay safe.

## Problem
People in India — especially elderly people — get calls about Aadhaar/PAN, KYC, blocked accounts, OTPs, fake police "digital arrests", prizes and investments. In the moment they cannot tell whether the caller is genuine, and the caller is pushing them to act now.

## User
A non-technical phone user, often elderly, who speaks Telugu, Hindi, English or a mix, and is mid-call with someone who feels wrong.

## The one action
Tap RUKO during the call → explain the situation (talk or type) → RUKO asks only questions that change the assessment → RUKO speaks what to do and a final assessment.

## Flow
1. Simulated incoming call (synthetic caller, voice generated by Timbre). Accept.
2. Call screen: caller, timer, Mute, Speaker, End, floating RUKO shield.
3. Tap RUKO → assistant sheet. Caller audio pauses. Two inputs only: a large mic button and a short text box. Optional language picker (Auto by default).
4. RUKO: Prisma transcribes → risk engine → one short tip + one follow-up question, spoken by Timbre, with a caption.
5. Repeat (max 3 follow-ups; never repeats a question).
6. Final assessment screen + speech: level, main reason, what to do; 1930 + cybercrime.gov.in for high risk / money lost.
7. Back to call (caller resumes) or End & clear.

## Outcomes
- **HIGH RISK** — OTP/PIN/password or remote access requested; or money demanded with threats, urgency, authority claims or secrecy; or threat + authority + secrecy.
- **SUSPICIOUS** — any single warning sign without the above combinations.
- **INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION** — no warning signs found. Never "safe" or "genuine".

## Non-goals (v1)
Authentication, onboarding, caller-ID databases, banking/payments, call recording, auto-reporting, contacting family, dashboards, admin panels, real cellular call integration.

## Principles
Voice first; captions optional. Short replies. One question at a time. Calm palette, red only for high risk. Synthetic data only. No stored audio.
